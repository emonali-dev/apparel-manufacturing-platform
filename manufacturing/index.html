Process

Eight stages, tracked end to end.

Each production order carries its own size and colour breakdown, assigned team and quality status through every stage.

  1. Fabric sourcing & inspectionFabric is checked for weight, shade and defects before cutting.
  2. Pattern & samplingPatterns are developed and a sample approved before bulk.
  3. CuttingLays are planned and cut by size and colour breakdown.
  4. SewingPanels are assembled with consistent stitch density and seam finishing.
  5. Printing & embroideryDecoration is applied to approved placements and colours.
  6. Washing & finishingGarment washes, pressing and trimming bring the final hand feel.
  7. Quality inspectionMeasurements, workmanship and print are inspected against the approved sample.
  8. Packing & dispatchFolded, labelled and packed to your standard, ready for export.

Quality

Inspected against your approved sample.

Fabric is checked on arrival, workmanship is checked in-line, and finished goods are inspected for measurements, print and finish before packing.
