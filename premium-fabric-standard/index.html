Incoming quality control

No approval without evidence.

The following are proposed purchasing targets. The applicable test method, conditioning, sampling plan and product-specific limits must be agreed with the laboratory and written into the purchase order.

GSM
Target ±5% unless the purchase order specifies tighter limits
Dimensional change
Target ≤3% length and width after agreed laundering test
Wash colour fastness
Target grade 4 or better
Rubbing fastness
Target dry grade 4; wet grade 3–4 or better
Pilling
Target grade 4 or better under agreed test cycle
Spirality / skew
Target ≤5% after laundering, subject to style-specific approval
Composition
Laboratory verified; no undeclared fibre substitution
Shade consistency
Match approved lab dip / sealed master swatch

Supplier approval checklist

Every lot must pass these checks.

01

Supplier, mill, origin, fabric code and lot traceability recorded

02

Fibre composition declaration matches purchase order

03

GSM and usable width measured at agreed sampling points

04

Shade checked against approved lab dip or master sample

05

Holes, stains, slubs, barre, needle lines and contamination inspected

06

Hand feel, surface and finish match the approved swatch

07

Shrinkage, colourfastness and pilling reports reviewed as applicable

08

Organic / recycled / performance claims independently verified

09

Failed rolls segregated, photographed and reported

10

Final decision recorded: Approved, Conditional or Rejected
