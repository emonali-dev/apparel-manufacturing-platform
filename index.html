Manufacturing excellence

Eight stages. One accountable flow.

Every production order moves through the same tracked stages, with quality checks before anything ships.

Capabilities
  1. Fabric sourcing & inspectionFabric is checked for weight, shade and defects before cutting.
  2. Pattern & samplingPatterns are developed and a sample approved before bulk.
  3. CuttingLays are planned and cut by size and colour breakdown.
  4. SewingPanels are assembled with consistent stitch density and seam finishing.
  5. Printing & embroideryDecoration is applied to approved placements and colours.
  6. Washing & finishingGarment washes, pressing and trimming bring the final hand feel.
  7. Quality inspectionMeasurements, workmanship and print are inspected against the approved sample.
  8. Packing & dispatchFolded, labelled and packed to your standard, ready for export.

Our story

About SIDDIQIYA
We believe the best T-shirt is the one made with intent — the right fabric, the right weight, finished with care. SIDDIQIYA Apparel brings design, manufacturing and export together so brands can build collections with confidence.

Why choose us

Built for buyers who notice the details.

Premium Fabric Quality

Combed, organic and heavyweight cottons, plus performance knits — each checked for weight and shade before cutting.

Professional Manufacturing

Cutting, sewing, printing and finishing managed as one production flow with a named team per order.

Custom Design Solutions

From your artwork to full ODM development, with samples approved before any bulk production.

Quality Control

Fabric, in-line and final inspections against your approved sample and measurement spec.

Flexible MOQ

International Shipping

Export-ready packing, commercial documents and shipping arranged to your destination.

How it works

From first request to delivery.

Six clear steps, with a sample approved before any bulk production.

The full process
  1. Submit requirementsSend your styles, fabrics, quantities and artwork through a quotation request.
  2. Receive quotationOur sales team replies with pricing, MOQ, lead time and shipping terms.
  3. Approve sampleA pre-production sample is made to your specification for approval.
  4. Confirm productionPurchase order and payment terms are agreed and production is scheduled.
  5. Quality inspectionGoods are inspected against the approved sample before packing.
  6. International shippingOrders are packed, documented and shipped on the agreed Incoterms.
