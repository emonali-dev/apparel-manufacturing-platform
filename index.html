Manufacturing excellence

Nine stages. One accountable flow.

Every production order moves through the same tracked stages, with quality checks before anything ships.

Capabilities
  1. Design & requirementWe confirm the style, artwork, quantities, sizes and target delivery with you in writing.
  2. Fabric selectionComposition, GSM and colour are agreed; swatches or lab dips are shared for approval.
  3. SamplingPatterns are developed and a sample is made and approved before bulk production.
  4. CuttingLays are planned and cut by size and colour breakdown.
  5. Sewing & stitchingPanels are assembled with consistent stitch density and seam finishing.
  6. Printing & embroideryDecoration is applied to the approved placements, colours and techniques.
  7. Quality inspectionMeasurements, workmanship and decoration are checked against the approved sample.
  8. PackagingGarments are folded, labelled and packed to your standard.
  9. Shipment preparationCartons, packing list and commercial documents are prepared for dispatch.
